Home battery storage with Powerwall 3

Powerwall stores energy for later use. It can be part of a home energy plan that includes solar and backup power. Steadfast offers Powerwall 3 installation. Tell us what you want to keep running and how you use power at home.

Plan the loads you want to support

Start with the equipment that matters most during an outage. A refrigerator, lights and larger appliances have different power needs. Backup time depends on the system design, stored energy and the loads in use. Discuss those needs before choosing a system size.

Prepare for a Powerwall discussion

Share your property location, recent electric bills and details of any existing solar equipment. Photos of the electrical panel and possible equipment locations can help start the conversation. The team will need to review the property before confirming the installation scope.

Connect storage with your home energy goals

Ask how the proposed battery system will work with your current equipment. Discuss where it can be installed, how it will be controlled and what work is included. If you also plan a new roof or solar installation, mention that early so the projects can be considered together.

Contact Steadfast to discuss your property and confirm service availability. We are based in Henderson and serve projects in Nevada, California and Arizona.
