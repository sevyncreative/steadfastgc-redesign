Dedicated EV charging at home

Steadfast offers Tesla Wall Connector installation for home charging. A dedicated charger lets you plug in where you park. Tell us which vehicle you drive, where you park and how much driving you do on a typical day.

Plan the charger location and power supply

The charger needs a suitable location and electrical supply. Discuss the distance from the panel, cable reach and the route to the parking space. Charging speed depends on the vehicle, electrical capacity and installed setup. Confirm compatibility for the selected charger and vehicle.

Prepare for a home charging quote

Share your property location, vehicle model and photos of the parking area. Include a photo of the electrical panel if it is safe to do so. Mention whether you park indoors or outside and whether you may charge a second vehicle later.

Coordinate charging with other home work

Let the team know about planned garage work, solar or battery storage. Ask which electrical work, mounting and setup are included in the proposal. Discuss any needed panel changes before setting the installation scope. A clear plan helps place the charger where it is practical for everyday use.

Contact Steadfast to discuss your property and confirm service availability. We are based in Henderson and serve projects in Nevada, California and Arizona.
