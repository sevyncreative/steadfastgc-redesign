Tesla Solar Roof: Power and Beauty Combined

Transform your roof into a power source with the innovative Tesla Solar Roof. Designed to blend seamlessly with your home’s architecture, Tesla Solar Roof tiles are both durable and aesthetically pleasing. Enjoy the benefits of renewable energy while enhancing your home’s curb appeal. Serving the Henderson and Las Vegas areas, Steadfast Construction, LLC is your trusted partner for Tesla Solar Roof installations.

Why Choose Us?

With a commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, our experienced team ensures professional installation and optimal performance of your Tesla Solar Roof. We use the best materials and latest techniques to ensure your solar roof is both efficient and durable.

Comprehensive Tesla Solar Roof Services

From initial consultation to final installation, we offer a full range of Tesla Solar Roof services to meet your needs. Our team handles everything from design to the final touches, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

Key Services

Site Assessment: Detailed assessment of your property to determine the optimal placement and setup for your Tesla Solar Roof.

Custom Design: Customized design of your solar roof to maximize energy production and blend seamlessly with your home’s architecture.

Professional Installation: Expert installation of Tesla Solar Roof tiles, ensuring durability and performance.

Monitoring and Support: Ongoing monitoring and support to ensure your solar roof operates at peak efficiency.

Key Benefits

Beautiful Aesthetic: Produce your own clean energy with no aesthetic compromise to your home.

Easy Installation: Order Solar Roof in minutes, and we’ll take care of the rest, from design to installation.

Maximum Durability: Made with tempered glass, Solar Roof tiles are three times stronger than standard roofing tiles.

Extra Insulation: Keep your home cool with insulating underlayment and integrated air gaps.

All-Weather Protection: Combine Solar Roof with Powerwall for continuous power when the grid goes down.

25-Year Warranty: Enjoy a 25-year roof warranty covering design, material, weather, and workmanship.

Energy Independence: Combine Solar Roof with Powerwall to rely less on utility power and back up your home during a grid outage.

Increase in Property Value: Homes with Tesla Solar Roof installations can see an increase in property value due to the energy savings and aesthetic appeal.
