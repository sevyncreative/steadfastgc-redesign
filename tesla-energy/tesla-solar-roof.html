Roofing and solar generation together

Tesla Solar Roof combines roofing with solar energy generation. Steadfast is a Tesla Solar Roof Certified Installer. We can discuss a Solar Roof project alongside the needs of your existing roof and electrical system.

Plan the roof before selecting the system

Roof shape, shade and usable roof area affect a solar roofing project. The condition of the existing roof also matters. Discuss the roof covering, any needed roof repairs and the electrical work as part of the same plan.

Prepare for a Solar Roof consultation

Share your property location, recent electric bills and any roof records you have. Tell us the age of the roof if known. Photos taken from the ground can help explain its shape and surroundings. Mention planned additions or other work that could change the roof.

Consider solar and storage as one plan

If backup power is a goal, ask about pairing solar with a battery. Discuss which loads you want to support and what the proposed system can do. Request the current product specifications, written warranty terms and project scope before making a decision. Output and savings depend on the property and energy use.

Contact Steadfast to discuss your property and confirm service availability. We are based in Henderson and serve projects in Nevada, California and Arizona.
