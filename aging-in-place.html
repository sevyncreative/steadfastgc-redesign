Start with the daily tasks that need to be easier

Accessible home changes should fit the people who use the space. Tell us where movement, reach or balance creates a challenge. A project may focus on one doorway or include several rooms and the path between them.

Consider the entrance, bathroom and kitchen as part of the same route. Note the use of walkers, wheelchairs or other aids. If a care professional has given guidance, share it during planning so the proposed changes can reflect those needs.

Plan home access in practical stages

List the changes needed now and those you may want later. This helps keep the scope focused. Choose a service above for more details, then contact Steadfast with your location, photos and priorities. The team can discuss the next step and confirm service availability.
