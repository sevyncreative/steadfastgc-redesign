New drywall and wall repairs

Steadfast installs drywall for new spaces and renovations. We also repair holes, cracks and damaged sections. Tell us whether you need a small patch, work across a full room or new walls as part of a larger project.

Plan the drywall finish

A repair needs to fit the surrounding wall as well as fill the damaged area. Discuss texture, seams and the finish expected before painting. If damage followed a leak, explain whether the source of the water has been addressed.

Details for a drywall quote

Share close-up photos and a view of the whole wall or ceiling. Note the size and location of the damage. Let us know about trim, cabinets or other features nearby, and whether painting is part of the requested scope.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.
