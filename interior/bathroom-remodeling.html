Remodel a bathroom around daily use

Steadfast offers bathroom remodeling, including fixtures, tile, flooring and storage. Your project may refresh the current room or change its layout. Start with what works today and what you would like to improve.

Choose the bathroom layout and finishes

Think about shower or tub use, storage, lighting and space around fixtures. Moving fixtures can change the scope of the work. Discuss flooring and wall finishes together so the room feels consistent. Share any access needs early in the planning process.

Prepare for your bathroom remodel

Send photos of the room and note which fixtures you want to keep. Tell us whether this is your only bathroom and how you use it. A list of priorities helps separate the changes you need from optional upgrades.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.
