Window and door repair or replacement

Steadfast offers window and door repairs, replacement and installation. Work can address worn parts, drafts or a change in appearance. Tell us which openings need attention and whether you want to keep the current sizes.

Choose windows and doors for your space

Think about how each unit opens, the room around it and the finish on both sides. Discuss glass, frames, trim and hardware as part of the same scope. A larger opening may need more work than a like-for-like replacement.

Prepare a window or door request

Send photos from inside and outside where possible. Include rough sizes and note sticking, gaps or visible damage. Tell us which rooms are involved and whether security, comfort, light or ease of use is your main goal.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.

Explore wider doorways for easier access.

Ask how new units will meet the existing wall finish and trim. Confirm whether the proposal includes removal of old units, hardware and finish repairs. If you want to match other windows or doors, share clear photos of those too.
