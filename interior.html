Plan changes around the way you live

A remodel starts with what you need from the room. More storage, easier movement and worn finishes each call for different work. Choose a service above to explore the options and learn what to share with the team.

Tell us which parts of the space you want to keep. Cabinets, appliances, flooring and trim can all affect the scope. If you are changing more than one room, describe how the spaces connect and how you will use the home during the work.

Prepare for an interior project discussion

Photos, rough dimensions and a short list of priorities are useful starting points. Include your preferred timing and any access needs. Contact Steadfast to discuss your property, or view our kitchen renovation gallery for examples of work in progress and finished details.
