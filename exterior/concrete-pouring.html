Concrete for paths, patios and pads

Steadfast provides concrete pouring for driveways, paths, patios and pads. New concrete starts with a clear plan for how the surface will be used. A patio for foot traffic and a driveway for vehicles have different needs.

Planning a concrete surface

Discuss the size, finish and edges of the new surface. Note nearby doors, drains and planting beds. Ask how site preparation, removal of old material and cleanup will fit into the scope. Confirm when the surface can be used after the pour.

Details for a concrete quote

Share photos of the site and rough dimensions if you have them. Tell us how crews can reach the area and whether an existing slab needs removal. Explain any pooling water or cracks you have seen.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.
