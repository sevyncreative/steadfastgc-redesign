Stucco repairs and exterior finishes

Steadfast offers stucco application, repair and finishing. Work can focus on a damaged area or a larger exterior surface. Share whether you want to repair cracks, refresh a finish or plan stucco as part of a renovation.

Choose a stucco texture and color

The base surface, texture and color all affect the finished result. Discuss preparation before the finish is applied. For patch work, ask how the repaired area will blend with older stucco. Existing weathering can affect the match.

Prepare for stucco work

Send photos of the whole wall and close views of cracks or worn areas. Note any moisture concerns and whether the same spot has been repaired before. Include your preferred finish and any nearby areas that also need work.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.

Read about siding repair.

For a whole-wall finish, discuss how corners, trim and nearby surfaces will be treated. Ask whether the scope includes removal of loose material and cleanup. A sample texture or color reference can help explain the result you want.
