Driveway and sidewalk repairs

Cracks, worn surfaces and uneven paths can make access harder. Steadfast offers new driveways and sidewalks as well as repairs. We can discuss concrete, asphalt and pavers based on the area and the work you need.

Plan for access during driveway work

Think about where you will park and how people will reach the front door. Discuss slopes, drainage and the point where the new surface meets the street or garage. Confirm which areas need repair and which may need replacement.

What to share about your path or driveway

Send photos of damage and a wider view of the site. Include rough dimensions, your preferred material and any access concerns. Let us know if the path serves a home, a business or a shared space.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.

Read about concrete pouring.

Discuss the edges where new work meets older paving. Ask whether the proposal includes removal, base preparation and cleanup. If people still need to use part of the route, agree on how access will be maintained while the work is underway.
