Wider openings for easier movement

A narrow doorway can make moving between rooms difficult with a walker or wheelchair. Steadfast offers doorway widening as part of accessible home renovations. Tell us which openings are hard to use and how you move through the home.

Plan the full route through a doorway

The space beside a door matters as well as the opening itself. Discuss the approach, door swing and room to turn. Wall construction, nearby switches and trim can affect the work. Ask what changes are included beyond the new door and frame.

Prepare for doorway widening

Send photos of both sides of the opening and rough measurements if available. Note the mobility device used and other tight points along the route. Tell us whether you need one doorway changed or a plan across several rooms.

Steadfast is based in Henderson and serves Las Vegas, with projects across Nevada, California and Arizona. Contact our team with your location to confirm service availability and discuss the next step.
