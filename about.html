At Steadfast Construction, LLC, we are a versatile and skilled general contractor serving Henderson and Las Vegas, with services across Nevada, California, and Arizona. We bring over a decade of experience to residential renovations, commercial construction, roofing, siding, and more.

Our mission is straightforward: deliver top-quality construction that meets and exceeds our clients’ expectations, no matter the size or complexity of the project.
